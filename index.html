More than a drink.

Boba Bloom started with a simple craving: a bubble tea counter in Cotonou that took flavor as seriously as it took fun. No powdered mixes, no shortcuts — just real brewed tea, fresh fruit, and milk whisked to order.

Every batch of tapioca is cooked fresh through the day, so the pearls you get are always soft, chewy, and never sitting around. Our recipes mix classic tea culture with the flavors Cotonou already loves — mango, passion fruit, brown sugar, and matcha grown for its actual taste, not just its color.

We built Boba Bloom for the after-school catch-ups, the study breaks, and the group chats that turn into group orders. One cup, endless combinations, and a reason to smile on the way out.
